# 4-Week Mesocycle: Upper / Lower / Back Progressive Overload

TrainHeroic-inspired strength and hypertrophy block for Jennifer Nixon. Five lifting days (Monday–Friday), optional Saturday cardio, Sunday rest and review.

Lower-body days fall on **Monday, Wednesday, and Friday**, each with a different emphasis so the week stays recovery-friendly: squat strength, posterior-chain strength, then a moderate full-lower and core session. **Tuesday** is upper-body push (bench, incline and overhead press, shoulders, triceps). **Thursday** is back and pull (rows, chin-ups or pulldowns, rear delts, biceps).

This mesocycle borrows the session shape and progression ideas used in popular TrainHeroic marketplace programs — especially Rick Gorrell’s BUILT 5-day Push/Pull/Legs + Upper/Lower (mobility/prep, compound primary, double-progression accessories, 40–60 minute sessions), Jeremy Lezama’s 5D Hypertrophy/Strength (Lower / Push / Pull / Lower mix, movement-specific prep and a short cooldown stretch), and Daniel Lemke’s 4-week accumulation blocks (Week 1 at 3 RIR, Week 2 at 2 RIR, Week 3 at 1–2 RIR, Week 4 reduced volume). It is an original program written to that style, not a copy of a paid TrainHeroic plan.

Read `README.md` for progression rules, substitutions, and how daily logging works. Use `progress.json` for live working weights. Log each day in `logs/YYYY-MM-DD.md`.

## How to read a session

Each lifting day follows the same TrainHeroic-style layout:

1. **Warm-Up / Prep** — general pulse raiser plus movement-specific activation. For quality, not fatigue.
2. **A. Primary lift** — the overload target for the day. Ramp up, then complete the prescribed work sets at that week’s RPE/RIR.
3. **B. Secondary lift** — the supporting compound. Double progression in a rep range unless a fixed-rep strength scheme is written.
4. **C / D. Accessory supersets** — paired work with shorter rest. Stay in the rep range at the week’s RIR.
5. **E. Finisher / Core** — a short pump or trunk piece. Friday carries the main core finisher. Cut this first if time is tight on other days.

**Ramp-up example (primary lifts):** empty bar or a very light set × 8–10, about 50% of the work weight × 5, about 70% × 3, then a heavy single or triple at about 90%. Then start logged work sets.

**Rest:** Primaries 2.5–3.5 minutes (squat and hinge can take the full 3–3.5). Secondaries about 2 minutes. Supersets 75–90 seconds after the pair. Finishers 45–75 seconds.

**Unilateral work:** Do both sides. Start with the weaker side if you know it.

Weights are left open on purpose until Week 1 working weights are logged. Pick the load that matches the written RPE/RIR with clean technique.

## Week 1 — Establish

**Target:** Find honest working weights. Primaries @ **RPE 7 (3 RIR)**. Accessories @ **2–3 RIR**, starting at the low-to-mid end of the rep range. Technique first.

### Monday — Lower Body — Quad-Focused Strength

**Warm-Up / Prep** (5–8 minutes)

- 5 minutes easy bike
- World’s greatest stretch: 1 × 5 / side
- Heels-elevated goblet squat: 2 × 8–10, moderate load, tight brace
- Anterior tibialis raises: 1 × 12–15
- Banded lateral walks: 1 × 10 steps / side, stay in a quarter squat
- Squat ramp-up sets

**A. Back Squat** — Primary  
4 × 6 @ RPE 7 (3 RIR). Rest 3–3.5 minutes.  
*Swap: goblet, safety-bar, or hack squat, or a squat-stance leg press if needed.*

**B. Leg Press (feet lower on the platform, quad stance)**  
3 × 10–12 @ 2–3 RIR. Rest 2 minutes.

**C1. Leg Extension** — 3 × 10–15 @ 2–3 RIR  
**C2. Seated or Lying Leg Curl** — 3 × 10–12 @ 2–3 RIR  
Superset. Rest 90 seconds after the pair. Keep the curl moderate; Wednesday is the main hamstring day.

**D. Walking Lunges**  
2 × 10–12 / leg @ 2–3 RIR. Bodyweight or light dumbbells. Skip if the squat and leg press already felt like enough.

**E. Finisher**

- Standing calf raise: 3 × 10–15 @ 2–3 RIR, full stretch at the bottom, pause at the top
- Side plank: 2 × 20–30 seconds / side

---

### Tuesday — Upper Body — Push, Shoulders & Arms

**Warm-Up / Prep** (5–8 minutes)

- 5 minutes easy bike, row, or walk
- Band pull-aparts: 2 × 15
- Face pulls with external rotation: 2 × 12
- Side-lying open books: 1 × 8 / side
- Scapular push-ups then regular push-ups: 1 × 8
- Light dumbbell lateral raises: 1 × 12–15
- Bench press ramp-up sets

**A. Barbell Bench Press** — Primary  
4 × 6 @ RPE 7 (3 RIR). Rest 2.5–3.5 minutes.  
*Swap: dumbbell bench or machine chest press, same sets and RPE.*

**B. Seated or Standing Dumbbell or Barbell Overhead Press** — Secondary  
3 × 8–10 @ 2–3 RIR. Rest 2 minutes.  
Dumbbells are a good default if shoulder comfort is a concern. Do not lean back into a standing incline bench press.

**C1. Incline Dumbbell or Machine Press** — 3 × 8–12 @ 2–3 RIR  
**C2. Dumbbell Lateral Raise** — 3 × 12–15 @ 2–3 RIR  
Superset. Rest 75–90 seconds after the pair. Soft elbows on laterals; raise the upper arm, do not swing.

**D1. Overhead Triceps Extension or Skull Crusher** — 3 × 8–12 @ 2–3 RIR  
**D2. Triceps Pushdown** — 2–3 × 12–15 @ 2–3 RIR  
Superset. Rest 75 seconds after the pair.

**E. Finisher (optional pump)**

- Pec deck or cable fly: 2 × 12–15 @ 2–3 RIR
- Optional lean-away or cable lateral raise: 1–2 × 12–15

If time is short, skip E. Leave pull work for Thursday.

### Wednesday — Lower Body — Posterior Chain / Glutes & Hamstrings

**Warm-Up / Prep** (5–8 minutes)

- 5 minutes easy bike or row
- 90/90 breathing or dead bug: 1 × 6 slow breaths or 6 / side
- Glute bridge: 2 × 10, squeeze at the top; second set a little more explosive
- Banded clamshells: 1 × 12–15 / side
- Tall-kneeling or half-kneeling Pallof hold: 1 × 20–30 seconds / side
- Hip-hinge drill with a dowel or empty bar: 8 slow reps
- RDL ramp-up sets

**A. Romanian Deadlift** — Primary  
4 × 6 @ RPE 7 (3 RIR). Rest 3–3.5 minutes.  
Soft knees, push the hips back, keep the bar close, and stop when the hamstrings are loaded without the back rounding.  
*Swap: trap-bar RDL or conventional deadlift in a 4 × 5 scheme at the same RPE if that is the hinge you already train well.*

**B. Barbell or Machine Hip Thrust**  
3 × 8–12 @ 2–3 RIR. Rest 2 minutes. Pause briefly at the top with a glute squeeze. Chin tucked, ribs down.

**C1. Lying or Seated Leg Curl** — 3 × 10–12 @ 2–3 RIR  
**C2. Step-Up or Reverse Lunge** — 3 × 8–10 / leg @ 2–3 RIR  
Superset. Rest 90 seconds after the pair.

**D. 45° Back Extension**  
3 × 10–15 @ 2–3 RIR. Drive with the glutes and hamstrings; do not hyperextend the finish.

**E. Light trunk work (keep the main core finisher for Friday)**

- Pallof press: 2 × 8–10 / side
- Optional hollow hold: 1–2 × 20–30 seconds

---

### Thursday — Back & Pull Focus

**Warm-Up / Prep** (5–8 minutes)

- 5 minutes easy row
- Band or cable Y / T / W: 1 × 10 each
- Lat pullover (band or cable): 1 × 12
- Shoulder external and internal rotation: 1 × 12 / side
- Half-kneeling rotational row: 1 × 8 / side
- Dead hang: 1 × 20–30 seconds
- Row ramp-up sets

**A. Barbell Bent-Over Row or Chest-Supported T-Bar Row** — Primary  
4 × 6–8 @ RPE 7 (3 RIR). Rest 2.5–3 minutes.  
Prefer chest-supported if your lower back is still tired from Wednesday’s RDL. Keep the torso locked and pull to the lower ribs or mid-chest, not a shrug.

**B. Weighted Chin-Up, Pull-Up, or Neutral-Grip Lat Pulldown** — Secondary  
4 × 6–8 @ RPE 7. Rest 2 minutes.  
Use assistance or a pulldown if you cannot complete 6 clean reps. Add load only if 8 reps at RPE 7 is easy.

**C1. Single-Arm Dumbbell or Cable Row** — 3 × 8–12 / side @ 2–3 RIR  
**C2. Straight-Arm Lat Pulldown or Dumbbell Pullover** — 3 × 10–12 @ 2–3 RIR  
Superset. Rest 75–90 seconds after the pair.

**D1. Face Pull or Rear Delt Fly** — 3 × 12–15 @ 2–3 RIR  
**D2. Hammer Curl or EZ-Bar / Dumbbell Curl** — 3 × 8–12 @ 2–3 RIR  
Superset. Rest 75 seconds after the pair.

**E. Finisher**

- Farmer carry or suitcase carry: 3 × 30–40 meters (or 20–30 seconds), tall posture
- Optional easy dead hang: 1 × 20–30 seconds

---

### Friday — Lower Body — Full Lower & Core

This is the moderate lower day: a different squat pattern, unilateral work, a light hinge, and the week’s main core finisher. It should not feel like a third heavy strength session.

**Warm-Up / Prep** (5–8 minutes)

- 5 minutes easy bike
- World’s greatest stretch: 1 × 5 / side
- Goblet squat: 2 × 8, moderate load
- Hip-flexor or couch-stretch position: 20–30 seconds / side
- Glute bridge or bird dog: 1 × 8 / side
- Front-squat or goblet ramp-up sets

**A. Front Squat or Goblet Squat** — Primary  
4 × 8 @ RPE 7 (3 RIR). Rest 2.5–3 minutes.  
Stay tall, elbows up on a front squat, and use a goblet or safety-bar squat if the rack position is uncomfortable. This is a quality pattern day, not a max-effort back-squat clone.

**B. Bulgarian Split Squat (rear-foot elevated)**  
3 × 8–10 / leg @ 2–3 RIR. Rest about 90 seconds per leg. Dumbbells at the sides. A slightly more upright torso keeps this balanced rather than a pure hinge.

**C1. Single-Leg RDL or Cable Pull-Through** — 3 × 8–10 / side (or 3 × 10–12 pull-throughs) @ 2–3 RIR  
**C2. Seated Calf Raise or Hip Adduction / Abduction** — 3 × 10–15 @ 2–3 RIR  
Superset. Rest 75–90 seconds after the pair. Keep the hinge lighter than Wednesday’s RDL.

**D. Core finisher circuit** — 2–3 rounds, about 45–60 seconds between rounds

- Cable or weighted crunch: 10–15
- Pallof press: 8–10 / side
- Hollow hold (20–30 seconds) or dead bug (6–8 / side)
- Optional side plank or Copenhagen plank: 20–30 seconds / side

---

### Saturday — Optional Cardio & Conditioning

Choose **one**. Skip or shorten this day if Friday left you very sore, sleep was poor, or you feel run down.

**Option A — Zone 2 Steady State (default)**  
30–45 minutes easy bike, incline walk, row, or easy jog. Conversational pace (about RPE 4–6 out of 10). You should be able to speak in full sentences.

**Option B — Intervals (only if recovered and you want a harder day)**

- 5 minutes easy warm-up
- 6 × 30 seconds hard / 90 seconds easy (bike, row, or hill walk)
- 5 minutes easy cool-down and a light stretch

**Option C — Mixed**  
20 minutes Zone 2 plus 10 minutes easy mobility.

Cooldown for all options: 5 minutes easy moving, then any tight areas for 30 seconds per side (hip flexors, hamstrings, pecs, lats).

---

### Sunday — Rest / Mobility & Weekly Review

Rest from hard training. Optional 20–30 minute mobility flow, all for quality:

- 90/90 breathing, feet on a wall if you like: 5 slow breaths (inhale about 5 seconds, exhale 5–8)
- Cat-cow: 8 reps
- World’s greatest stretch: 5 / side
- Pigeon or elevated pigeon: 30 seconds / side
- Couch stretch: 30 seconds / side
- Thoracic open books: 6 / side
- PVC or broom pass-throughs: 10
- Deep squat hold: 30–45 seconds
- Optional easy walk: 10–20 minutes

**Weekly review — write this in `logs/YYYY-MM-DD.md` and `progress.json`:**

- Which sessions were completed or missed?
- Which lifts were strong, and which working weights should be entered?
- Any pain, ugly technique, or stalls?
- Sleep, stress, and energy across the week (1–5)
- Did the three lower days recover reasonably, or should Friday stay even lighter?
- What load or rep changes does Week 2 call for?

## Week 3 — Overload

**Target:** Highest productive week of the block, not a max-out week. If Week 2 was on target, add another small load jump on primaries, or keep the load and hold quality reps. Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday primaries @ **RPE 8.5–9 (1–2 RIR)**. Friday’s front squat stays at 4 × 6–8 @ **RPE 8–8.5** rather than a heavy 5 × 5, so squat and hinge recovery stay intact. Accessories: push toward the **top of the rep range** @ about 1–2 RIR. Sleep, food, and the written rest periods matter more this week.

### Monday — Lower Body — Quad-Focused Strength

**Warm-Up / Prep:** Same as Week 1. Do not skip ramp-up sets.

**A. Back Squat** — 5 × 5 @ RPE 8.5–9 (1–2 RIR). Rest 3–3.5 minutes.  
**B. Leg Press** — 3 × 10–12, top of the range if form holds  
**C1. Leg Extension** — 3 × 10–15  
**C2. Leg Curl** — 3 × 10–12  
**D.** Walking lunges 2 × 10–12 / leg, or skip if the squat already took more out of you.  
**E.** Standing calf raise 3 × 12–15; side plank 2 × 30–40 seconds / side.

### Tuesday — Upper Body — Push, Shoulders & Arms

**Warm-Up / Prep:** Same as Week 1.

**A. Barbell Bench Press** — 5 × 5 @ RPE 8.5–9 (1–2 RIR). Rest 3–3.5 minutes.  
**B. Overhead Press** — 3 × 8–10, toward 10 @ 1–2 RIR. If Week 2 hit all sets of 10 at 2 RIR, take a small load jump and work 8–10 clean reps.  
**C1. Incline Dumbbell or Machine Press** — 3 × 8–12, toward 10–12  
**C2. Lateral Raise** — 3 × 12–15  
**D1. Overhead Triceps Extension or Skull Crusher** — 3 × 8–12  
**D2. Triceps Pushdown** — 3 × 12–15  
**E.** Optional pump as in Week 2. Isolation work may come closer to failure; keep the bench and overhead press inside the written RPE.

### Wednesday — Lower Body — Posterior Chain / Glutes & Hamstrings

**Warm-Up / Prep:** Same as Week 1. Be honest about lower-back freshness after Monday’s squat.

**A. Romanian Deadlift** — 4 × 5 @ RPE 8.5–9 (1–2 RIR). Rest 3–3.5 minutes.  
Do not grind a rounded-back rep. If the hinge feels off, keep Week 2’s load and take quality sets.  
**B. Hip Thrust** — 3 × 8–12, toward 12 @ 1–2 RIR  
**C1. Leg Curl** — 3 × 10–12  
**C2. Step-Up or Reverse Lunge** — 3 × 8–10 / leg  
**D.** Back extension 3 × 10–15.  
**E.** Pallof press 2 × 8–10 / side; optional hollow hold 1–2 × 20–30 seconds.

### Thursday — Back & Pull Focus

**Warm-Up / Prep:** Same as Week 1.

**A. Barbell or Chest-Supported T-Bar Row** — 4 × 6 @ RPE 8.5–9 (1–2 RIR). Rest 2.5–3 minutes.  
If Week 2 already hit 4 × 8 at RPE 8, take the small load jump and perform 4 × 6 at the new weight.  
**B. Weighted Chin-Up, Pull-Up, or Pulldown** — 4 × 5–8 @ RPE 8.5. Rest 2 minutes.  
**C1. Single-Arm Row** — 3 × 8–12 / side, toward 12  
**C2. Straight-Arm Pulldown or Pullover** — 3 × 10–12  
**D1. Face Pull or Rear Delt Fly** — 3 × 12–15  
**D2. Hammer or EZ-Bar / Dumbbell Curl** — 3 × 8–12, toward 12  
**E.** Farmer or suitcase carry 3 × 30–40 meters, slightly heavier if posture stays tall.

### Friday — Lower Body — Full Lower & Core

**Warm-Up / Prep:** Same as Week 1. If Monday’s squat and Wednesday’s RDL left you flat, hold Friday’s front-squat load at the Week 2 number and stay at RPE 8.

**A. Front Squat or Goblet Squat** — 4 × 6–8 @ RPE 8–8.5 (1.5–2 RIR). Rest 2.5–3 minutes.  
If Week 2 hit all sets of 8 at RPE 8 and you feel recovered, add a small load jump and work 6–8 clean reps. Do not turn this into a fifth heavy 5 × 5.  
**B. Bulgarian Split Squat** — 3 × 8–10 / leg, aim for 10 @ 1–2 RIR  
**C1. Single-Leg RDL or Cable Pull-Through** — 3 × 8–10 / side (or 3 × 10–12)  
**C2. Seated Calf Raise or Hip Adduction / Abduction** — 3 × 10–15  
**D.** Core circuit 3 rounds, as in Week 1.

### Saturday — Optional Cardio & Conditioning

Prefer **Option A: 30–40 minutes Zone 2**. If recovery is clearly good, Option B may be **8 × 30 seconds hard / 90 seconds easy**, or 8 × 40 / 80 if Week 2 intervals felt controlled. When in doubt, choose Zone 2 or take the day off. This is not the week to add a long hard conditioning session on top of the overload lifting.

### Sunday — Rest / Mobility & Weekly Review

Same mobility menu, and take the rest seriously. Review:

- Which primaries are ready for a Week 4 realization AMRAP?
- Any lift that should be held or substituted rather than tested?
- How did the three lower days feel in the overload week?
- Soreness, sleep, and readiness heading into the deload

## Week 4 — Deload + Realization

**Target:** Cut accumulated fatigue, then take a conservative AMRAP on each primary to set working weights for the next mesocycle. Volume is reduced about 40–50%. Work sets @ **RPE 7**. The last working set of each **primary only** may be an AMRAP **stopped at 2–3 RIR** — not a true max, no missed reps, no form breakdown. Accessories: 2 sets at the low end of the range @ RPE 7. No intervallic Saturday.

### Monday — Lower Body — Quad-Focused Strength

**Warm-Up / Prep:** Same as Week 1, including ramp-up sets.

**A. Back Squat** — 3 × 5 @ RPE 7. On the **last set only**, continue to an AMRAP stopped at 2–3 RIR. Rest 3–3.5 minutes.  
**B. Leg Press** — 2 × 10 @ RPE 7  
**C1. Leg Extension** — 2 × 10 @ RPE 7  
**C2. Leg Curl** — 2 × 10 @ RPE 7  
**D.** Skip walking lunges this week.  
**E.** Standing calf raise 2 × 10–12; side plank 1 × 20–30 seconds / side.

### Tuesday — Upper Body — Push, Shoulders & Arms

**Warm-Up / Prep:** Same as Week 1.

**A. Barbell Bench Press** — 3 × 5 @ RPE 7. On the **last set only**, AMRAP to 2–3 RIR. Rest 2.5–3.5 minutes.  
**B. Overhead Press** — 2 × 8 @ RPE 7  
**C1. Incline Dumbbell or Machine Press** — 2 × 8–10 @ RPE 7  
**C2. Lateral Raise** — 2 × 12 @ RPE 7  
**D1. Overhead Triceps Extension or Skull Crusher** — 2 × 8–10 @ RPE 7  
**D2. Triceps Pushdown** — 2 × 12 @ RPE 7  
**E.** Skip the pump finisher this week.

### Wednesday — Lower Body — Posterior Chain / Glutes & Hamstrings

**Warm-Up / Prep:** Same as Week 1.

**A. Romanian Deadlift** — 3 × 5 @ RPE 7. On the **last set only**, AMRAP to 2–3 RIR, stopping immediately if the back rounds or the hinge shortens. Rest 3–3.5 minutes.  
**B. Hip Thrust** — 2 × 8–10 @ RPE 7  
**C1. Leg Curl** — 2 × 10 @ RPE 7  
**C2. Step-Up or Reverse Lunge** — 2 × 8 / leg @ RPE 7  
**D.** Back extension 2 × 10, light, or skip.  
**E.** Pallof press 1–2 × 8 / side, or skip and save trunk work for Friday.

### Thursday — Back & Pull Focus

**Warm-Up / Prep:** Same as Week 1.

**A. Barbell or Chest-Supported T-Bar Row** — 3 × 6 @ RPE 7. On the **last set only**, AMRAP to 2–3 RIR. Rest 2.5–3 minutes.  
**B. Weighted Chin-Up, Pull-Up, or Pulldown** — 3 × 6–8 @ RPE 7. Rest 2 minutes.  
**C1. Single-Arm Row** — 2 × 8 / side @ RPE 7  
**C2. Straight-Arm Pulldown or Pullover** — 2 × 10 @ RPE 7  
**D1. Face Pull or Rear Delt Fly** — 2 × 12 @ RPE 7  
**D2. Hammer or EZ-Bar / Dumbbell Curl** — 2 × 8 @ RPE 7  
**E.** Farmer or suitcase carry 2 × 30 meters, moderate load.

### Friday — Lower Body — Full Lower & Core

**Warm-Up / Prep:** Same as Week 1.

**A. Front Squat or Goblet Squat** — 3 × 8 @ RPE 7. On the **last set only**, AMRAP to 2–3 RIR. Rest 2.5–3 minutes.  
**B. Bulgarian Split Squat** — 2 × 8 / leg @ RPE 7  
**C1. Single-Leg RDL or Cable Pull-Through** — 2 × 8 / side @ RPE 7  
**C2. Seated Calf Raise or Hip Adduction / Abduction** — 2 × 10–12 @ RPE 7  
**D.** Core circuit 1–2 easy rounds.

### Saturday — Optional Cardio & Conditioning

**Zone 2 only:** 20–30 minutes easy bike, incline walk, or row. No intervals this week. Full skip is also a good choice if you want a complete rest day before the weekly review.

### Sunday — Rest / Mobility & End-of-Block Review

Same optional mobility flow as Week 1, then a fuller review before Mesocycle 2:

- Sessions completed across the 4 weeks, including misses
- Primary AMRAP results (load × reps @ estimated RIR) and the working weights they suggest for the next block
- Accessories that earned a load jump via double progression
- How the Monday / Wednesday / Friday lower split felt, and whether Friday should stay moderate
- Lifts to keep, swap, or regress because of pain or technique
- Recovery pattern (sleep, stress, soreness)
- Decision: start Mesocycle 2 next Monday with updated loads, same split, and only small variation changes if needed

Do not begin another overload week the following day. The next Monday is the start of Mesocycle 2.

## Primary progression at a glance

| Day | Primary lift | Week 1 | Week 2 | Week 3 | Week 4 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Monday | Back Squat | 4 × 6 @ RPE 7 | 4 × 6 @ RPE 8 | 5 × 5 @ RPE 8.5–9 | 3 × 5 @ RPE 7, last set AMRAP to 2–3 RIR |
| Tuesday | Barbell Bench Press | 4 × 6 @ RPE 7 | 4 × 6 @ RPE 8 | 5 × 5 @ RPE 8.5–9 | 3 × 5 @ RPE 7, last set AMRAP to 2–3 RIR |
| Wednesday | Romanian Deadlift | 4 × 6 @ RPE 7 | 4 × 6 @ RPE 8 | 4 × 5 @ RPE 8.5–9 | 3 × 5 @ RPE 7, last set AMRAP to 2–3 RIR |
| Thursday | Barbell or Chest-Supported Row | 4 × 6–8 @ RPE 7 | 4 × 6–8 @ RPE 8 | 4 × 6 @ RPE 8.5–9 | 3 × 6 @ RPE 7, last set AMRAP to 2–3 RIR |
| Friday | Front Squat or Goblet Squat | 4 × 8 @ RPE 7 | 4 × 8 @ RPE 8 | 4 × 6–8 @ RPE 8–8.5 | 3 × 8 @ RPE 7, last set AMRAP to 2–3 RIR |

Load increases between Weeks 1–3 only when the previous week hit the target RPE with clean form. Week 4 AMRAPs stop at 2–3 RIR and are used to set the next mesocycle, not to test a one-rep max. Friday stays in a higher-rep, slightly easier RPE band in Week 3 so the three lower days do not stack into three heavy strength sessions.
